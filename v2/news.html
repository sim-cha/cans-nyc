September 8, 2026

Fines begin: DSNY starts ticketing buildings without official NYC Bins

The warning period ended on Labor Day. DSNY is now issuing summonses starting at $50 to properties with 1-9 residential units that set out trash in anything other than an official NYC Bin. The city says trash bins are in stock at every Home Depot in the five boroughs.

DSNY Press Release #26-035 (Aug 26) · NY1
September 7, 2026

Gothamist: ~750,000 small buildings face the new fines

Gothamist's enforcement-eve report: fines run $50 to $200 for repeat offenders; roughly 750,000 buildings are covered; and Council members say constituents struggled to find bins during the summer supply crunch. DSNY says the bins remain available for sale.

Gothamist
August 5, 2026

NYC Bin manufacturer Otto in involuntary bankruptcy

Waste Dive reports Otto Environmental Systems - holder of the city's bin contract - was placed in involuntary Chapter 7 bankruptcy in May by three creditors alleging $2.6M in debts. Court filings describe disputes over assets moved between companies set up by Otto's CEO. About 1.1 million NYC Bins have been purchased; the city had expected 3.39 million delivered by May 2026. Home Depot listings now carry the official bin under the Edge brand, and DSNY maintains supply is stable.

Waste Dive
May 2026

Reimbursement window for STAR homeowners closes with the warning period

Owners of one- and two-family homes who received the STAR exemption or credit in 2024 or 2025 were eligible for a reimbursement covering one 45-gallon bin. The deadline was extended through the first week of September 2026 and has now closed.

DSNY NYC Bins
June 1, 2026

Official NYC Bins become mandatory for trash at 1-9 unit buildings

The containerization program's next phase took effect: only official NYC Bins count for trash setout. A summer warning period ran through Labor Day while bin supply recovered from the manufacturer's troubles.
