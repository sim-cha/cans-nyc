The NYC containerization timeline

Last verified September 9, 2026

How we got here - and what's next

Last updated September 9, 2026

  • October 2023
    DSNY issues the RFP for an official city bin: rat-resistant, mechanized-truck compatible, and about $50 for the most common size.
  • July 8, 2024
    The official NYC Bin is unveiled. Manufacturer Otto Environmental Systems wins the concession agreement, keeping prices well below comparable retail bins.
  • November 12, 2024
    Containerization rule takes effect: buildings with 1-9 residential units (plus city agencies, nonprofits, houses of worship, and professional offices in residential buildings) must set out trash in rigid bins of 55 gallons or less with tight-fitting lids. Black bags on the curb are no longer allowed for these buildings.
  • January 3, 2025
    Fines begin for trash set out without a lidded bin: $50 first offense, $100 second, $200 third and beyond. Any secure-lidded bin still qualifies at this stage.
  • June 1, 2025
    The Manhattan Community District 9 pilot launches: stationary on-street "Empire Bins" for buildings with 31+ units (optional for 10-30 units) in West Harlem - the model for the coming citywide rollout to large buildings.
  • Fall 2025
    NYC Bins go on direct sale at bins.nyc. Supply problems follow: the manufacturer, Otto, is placed in involuntary bankruptcy in May 2026, and bins become hard to find for a stretch.
  • June 1, 2026
    Official NYC Bins become mandatory for trash at all 1-9 unit properties. A summer-long warning period replaces fines while supply recovers.
  • September 7, 2026
    Warning period ends (Labor Day). The STAR homeowner bin reimbursement window also closes this week.
  • September 8, 2026
    Full enforcement begins. DSNY starts issuing $50 summonses for trash set out in anything but an official NYC Bin.

What's coming for larger buildings

Buildings with 10-30 units will choose between official NYC Bins and stationary on-street Empire Bins. Buildings with 31+ units will be required to use Empire Bins - assigned to the building, opened by key card, and emptied by DSNY's new automated side-loading trucks. The citywide containerization program for these buildings is being phased in, with full implementation targeted by June 1, 2032.

Sources: DSNY Residential Containerization · DSNY Press Release #26-035 · Adopted rule (PDF)
