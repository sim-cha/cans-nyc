About cans.nyc

cans.nyc exists because New York's trash rules changed fast, and the official information is scattered across press releases, adopted rules, and FAQ pages. We pull it together in plain English: what the official NYC Bin mandate requires, who it covers, the dates that matter, and what happens if you ignore it.

Everything on this site is drawn from primary sources - DSNY rule pages, the adopted rule text, city press releases - plus reporting from outlets like Gothamist, NY1, and Waste Dive. Each page links its sources. Rules change, so always confirm specifics at nyc.gov before acting on anything you read here.
