NYC's trash bin mandate is now being enforced.

If your building has 1 to 9 residential units, the black-bag era is over. Trash must go out in an official NYC Bin - and fines started September 8, 2026.

Enforcement is live. Since Tuesday, September 8, 2026, DSNY issues summonses starting at $50 for trash set out in anything other than an official NYC Bin. The warning period ended September 7.

The rules at a glance

Who must comply
Buildings with 1-9 residential units
Plus city agencies, nonprofits, houses of worship, and professional offices in residential buildings that get DSNY collection. Roughly 750,000 buildings citywide.
What's required
Official NYC Bin for trash
Any other bin - even a good one with a lid - no longer counts for trash setout. Recycling and compost bins are still your choice.
Set-out time
After 6:00 PM
Bins go out after 6 PM, and everything must be at the curb by midnight the night before collection.
Fines
$50 / $100 / $200
First, second, and third-plus offenses. Enforcement began September 8, 2026.
Where to buy
Home Depot
Every NYC Home Depot stocks the trash bins. Order for pickup at homedepot.com/NYCBins or delivery via DoorDash, Instacart, or Uber Eats.
What it costs
$45-$55 per bin
25 gal $45.19 · 35 gal $47.25 · 45 gal $54.60 (prices as of May 2026). The city priced these below comparable retail bins.

The short version

  • Trash: official NYC Bin only. No bags, no other cans.
  • Compost: any sealed, clearly labeled bin of 55 gallons or less. The official NYC compost bin is optional.
  • Recycling: any labeled bin, or a clear plastic bag (blue bin optional).
  • Owners, not renters: the property owner or building manager is responsible for buying the bins - and for the fines.
  • 10+ units: bigger buildings are on a separate track (stationary on-street Empire Bins). See the timeline.
